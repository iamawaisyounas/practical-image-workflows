# Contributing

Please propose corrections and useful additions through issues or pull requests.

Include the exact official URL, date checked, usable free allowance, processing location, account requirements and a concrete publishing use case. Disclose ownership, employment, sponsorship or other affiliation. Use direct links and neutral descriptions.

Separate documented vendor claims from measurements. Benchmarks require input provenance, settings, dimensions, byte counts, environment and method. Never invent results or promise ranking gains. Preserve competing options when they serve readers.

Check relative links, table rendering and heading navigation. Do not submit private images, credentials or customer data. Contributors retain responsibility for the rights to their submitted material.
